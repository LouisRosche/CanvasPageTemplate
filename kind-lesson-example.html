🌿 The Simple Science

Here's what's happening:

During spring and summer, leaves are green because of chlorophyll—a special chemical that helps plants make food from sunlight. Chlorophyll is green, so leaves look green!

But here's the secret: Other colors are always hiding in the leaf—reds, oranges, and yellows. We just can't see them because the green chlorophyll covers them up.

In fall, when days get shorter and cooler:

  1. The tree stops making new chlorophyll
  2. The old chlorophyll breaks down and disappears
  3. Now we can finally see the other colors that were there all along!

Think of it like this: Imagine you're wearing a green jacket over a red shirt. When you take off the jacket (chlorophyll), everyone can finally see your red shirt (the hidden colors)!

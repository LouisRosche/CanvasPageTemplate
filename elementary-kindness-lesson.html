💕 Being Kind to Others 💕

A special lesson about kindness for Grade 3

This lesson will take about 20 minutes. You're going to do great!

📖 A Story About Kindness

Meet Maria. Maria is 8 years old, just like many of you!

One day at school, Maria saw something that made her feel sad. Her classmate Alex dropped his lunch tray in the cafeteria. Food went everywhere! Some kids started laughing.

Alex looked like he wanted to cry.

Maria had a choice. She could laugh with the other kids, or she could do something different.

What do you think Maria did?

Maria walked over to Alex with a smile. She said, "Don't worry! Everyone drops things sometimes. I'll help you clean up!"

She got napkins and helped Alex pick up the food. Another student saw Maria helping and came over to help too. Then another student joined. Soon, five kids were helping Alex!

Alex stopped feeling sad. He smiled and said, "Thank you, Maria. You're really kind!"

💙

🤔 Let's Think About This

Questions for You

Think about these questions. There are no wrong answers!

  • How do you think Alex felt when kids laughed at him?
  • How do you think Alex felt when Maria helped him?
  • Why do you think other kids started helping after they saw Maria?
  • Have you ever helped someone who was having a hard time?
  • Has someone ever been kind to you when you needed it?

You can think about these quietly, talk about them with a friend, or write your thoughts down. Whatever feels right for you!

💡 What Is Kindness?

Kindness is when we do something nice for someone else, even when nobody is watching or asking us to.

Examples of Kindness

  • Helping someone who dropped something
  • Sharing your crayons or markers
  • Saying nice things to someone who looks sad
  • Including someone who is alone at recess
  • Helping a classmate who doesn't understand the work
  • Saying "please" and "thank you"
  • Being a good listener when someone wants to talk
🌈

Here's something amazing: When you're kind to someone, it makes BOTH of you feel good! The person you help feels better, and YOU feel happy too. It's like magic! ✨

🎨 Your Turn: Kindness in Action!

Now it's time for YOU to think about kindness. Choose one activity that sounds fun to you:

Choice 1: Draw a Kindness Picture

Draw a picture of yourself being kind to someone. It can be something you did before, or something you want to do!

Don't forget to add lots of happy colors! 🖍️

Choice 2: Write a Kindness Story

Write 3-5 sentences about a time when:

  • Someone was kind to you, OR
  • You were kind to someone else

How did it make you feel? ✏️

Choice 3: Make a Kindness Plan

Think of 3 kind things you can do this week:

  • At school
  • At home
  • Anywhere else

Write them down or tell someone about your plan! 📝

Remember: There's no right or wrong choice! Pick what sounds fun to you. You can even do more than one if you want to! 😊

🌟 The Kindness Challenge

Here's a fun challenge for this week:

Try to do at least ONE kind thing each day!

It can be something small, like:

  • Smiling at someone
  • Holding the door open
  • Saying something nice
  • Helping without being asked

Keep track if you want to! You can make checkmarks for each day you do something kind. But the most important thing is just BEING kind. 💕

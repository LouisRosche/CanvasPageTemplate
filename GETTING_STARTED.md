# Getting Started with Universal Canvas Learning Suite
## Your First 30 Minutes

## 👋 Welcome!

You're about to explore a teaching framework built on three values:
1. **Student-Centeredness** - Students' experience comes first
2. **Kindness** - Learning should feel supportive, not stressful
3. **Precision** - Instructions must be crystal clear

This guide will help you get started in just 30 minutes.

## 🎯 What to Do Next (After Your First 30 Minutes)

### For Your First Real Lesson:

1. **Choose a topic** (start with something familiar, not brand new)

2. **Pick your template:**
   - `simple-teacher-template.html` for full 45-60 minute lesson
   - `quick-activity-template.html` for 15-20 minute activity

3. **Gather your content:**
   - What's the main concept?
   - What examples will you use?
   - What questions will you ask?
   - What success looks like?

4. **Customize the template:**
   - Replace ALL CAPS PLACEHOLDER TEXT
   - Delete sections you don't need
   - Add more sections by copy-pasting if needed
   - Keep the kind, encouraging language

5. **Test it:**
   - Open in your browser
   - Click through as a student would
   - Check on your phone (does it work?)
   - Fix anything confusing

6. **Share with one class:**
   - Start with your friendliest class
   - Ask for honest feedback
   - Watch how students use it
   - Take notes on what works

7. **Iterate:**
   - Adjust based on feedback
   - Make instructions clearer
   - Add more support where students struggled
   - Celebrate what worked!

## ❓ Common Questions

### "Do I need to know how to code?"
**No!** If you can use Google Docs, you can use this. Just replace text in the templates.

### "What if I want to change the colors or design?"
You can, but you don't need to. The designs are already student-friendly and accessible. Focus on content!

### "Can I use this with Google Classroom / Canvas / Schoology?"
Yes! These are just HTML files. Upload and share like any other file.

### "Do students need accounts or logins?"
No! They just open the file in a browser. Progress saves locally (optional).

### "What if I don't have 1:1 devices?"
- Print the pages (they print reasonably well)
- Use computer lab time
- Project and do activities together
- Hybrid: digital for some, paper for others

### "Is this really free forever?"
Yes. No subscriptions, no accounts, no catches. Open and free.

### "What if I get stuck?"
1. Check `TROUBLESHOOTING_GUIDE.md`
2. Look at example lessons for ideas
3. Ask a colleague who's used it
4. Start simple—you can always add complexity later

### "How long does it take to make a lesson?"
- First time: 1-2 hours (you're learning)
- After a few: 30-45 minutes
- Once you're comfortable: 20-30 minutes

It's faster than creating a PowerPoint from scratch because the structure is already there!

### "Can I modify the templates?"
Absolutely! These are yours to customize however you want. The templates are starting points.

## 💙 Remember the Why

As you create lessons, keep coming back to the three questions:

1. **Student-Centeredness:** Does this give students agency? Does it connect to their lives?

2. **Kindness:** Would I want my child to experience this? Does it feel supportive?

3. **Precision:** Will every student understand this clearly? Is there any ambiguity?

If you can answer yes to these, you're on the right track! 🌟

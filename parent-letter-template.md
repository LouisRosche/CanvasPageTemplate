## 📧 Sample Letter: Introducing Digital Learning Pages

**[Customize this letter for your class and send to parents at the beginning of the year or when you first use this tool]**

---

Dear Parents and Guardians,

I'm excited to share something new we'll be using in class this year: **digital learning pages** that make lessons clear, engaging, and accessible for every student.

### What Are These Learning Pages?

These are special web pages I create for each lesson. They're like interactive worksheets that students can use on any device—computer, tablet, or even phone. Each page includes:

- Clear learning goals written in student-friendly language
- Step-by-step activities that guide students through learning
- Helpful examples and explanations
- Choices for how students learn (reading, watching videos, interactive activities)
- Encouraging messages that build confidence

### Why Am I Using This?

I chose this approach because it:

✅ **Makes learning clearer** - Every student knows exactly what to do
✅ **Supports different learning styles** - Students can choose how they learn best
✅ **Works on any device** - Computer, tablet, or phone
✅ **Works offline** - Students can save and use without internet
✅ **Is kind and supportive** - Language that builds confidence, not stress

### What Does This Mean for Your Child?

Your child will receive links or files for lessons. They can:

- Work at their own pace
- Review content as many times as needed
- Get help when stuck without feeling embarrassed
- See their progress clearly
- Choose activities that match their comfort level

### Privacy & Safety

These pages are designed with student privacy in mind:

- **No tracking** - We don't collect browsing data
- **Progress saves locally** - On your child's device only
- **No accounts needed** - Students don't create logins
- **Teacher-controlled content** - I create all content myself

If we use any tools like Google Forms, those follow your school's existing privacy policies.

### How You Can Help

- **Ask your child** what they're learning - They can show you their learning page!
- **Encourage effort** - Remind them it's okay to need help or take time
- **Provide a device if possible** - But we have alternatives if needed
- **Contact me with questions** - I'm here to help!

### What If My Child Doesn't Have a Device?

Please let me know! I can provide:
- Printed versions of lessons
- Use of school computers during class
- Alternative assignments that work for your family

No student will be left behind due to technology access.

### Technical Requirements

Very simple!
- **Any web browser** (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)
- **Any device** (computer, tablet, phone)
- **Internet to download** (but works offline after that)
- **No special software needed**

## 📋 Parent FAQ Sheet

**[Include this with your letter or post on your class website]**

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Will my child need to create an account?**
A: No! They just open the file and start learning.

**Q: How much will this cost?**
A: Nothing! It's completely free.

**Q: What if we don't have a computer?**
A: Works on tablets and phones too! Or I can provide printed versions.

**Q: What if my child gets stuck?**
A: Every lesson has help built in, plus they can always ask me!

**Q: Can I see what they're learning?**
A: Absolutely! Ask your child to show you their learning page.

**Q: Is this replacing textbooks?**
A: Supplementing, not replacing. We use both.

**Q: What about assessments and grades?**
A: Some lessons include assessments. I'll always communicate about grades.

**Q: Who made these pages?**
A: I do! I create each one specifically for our class.

**Q: Are they accessible for students with disabilities?**
A: Yes! They're designed to work with screen readers and follow accessibility guidelines.

**Q: What if the file won't open?**
A: Contact me and I'll help troubleshoot or provide an alternative.

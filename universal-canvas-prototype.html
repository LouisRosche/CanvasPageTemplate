🔬 Station 1: Energy Simulation Investigation

Takes 25-30 minutes | 20 points
Your Mission: Use the PhET Energy Skate Park simulation to figure out how mass, velocity, and energy are connected.
📚 Vocabulary for Station 1 ▼
Kinetic Energy (KE)
The energy an object has because it's moving. Formula: KE = ½mv²
Example: A bowling ball rolling has kinetic energy
Potential Energy (PE)
Stored energy from position or condition that can become kinetic energy
Example: At the top of a hill, the skater has maximum PE
Energy Conservation
Energy cannot be created or destroyed, only transformed. Total energy stays constant
Example: PE and KE trade back and forth, but total energy stays same

🟢 Foundations: Building Understanding

Start here if you're new to energy concepts:

  • Watch the tutorial video first
  • Use the guided investigation worksheet with sentence starters
  • Focus on observing: What happens to the energy bars as the skater moves?
  • Complete 2 tests (mass effect and height effect)

🟡 Target: Grade-Level Expectations

Work at grade level:

  • Complete all 4 investigation tests
  • Collect quantitative data (record actual energy values)
  • Explain patterns you observe using scientific vocabulary
  • Calculate kinetic energy using KE = ½mv²

🔴 Extensions: Going Deeper

Challenge yourself:

  • Design your own investigation question
  • Create a mathematical model to predict energy values
  • Investigate: Does velocity or mass have a bigger impact on KE? Provide evidence
  • Compare energy conservation with and without friction - where does energy go?

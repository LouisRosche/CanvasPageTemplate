🌊 What is the Water Cycle?

The water cycle is water's never-ending journey! Water goes round and round from the ocean, to the sky, back to the land, and back to the ocean again!

The Water Cycle Journey!

☀️ ➡️ 💧 ➡️ ☁️ ➡️ 🌧️ ➡️ 🌊

Sun heats water ➡️ Water goes up ➡️ Makes clouds ➡️ Rain falls ➡️ Water goes back!

💙 Cool, right? Water doesn't disappear—it just goes on a trip and comes back! You're going to learn each step of this amazing journey! 🎉

🔢 The 4 Steps of the Water Cycle

Let's learn each step! These big words might look tricky, but we'll learn them together!

☀️💧

Step 1: Evaporation

Say it: ee-VAP-or-AY-shun

What happens: The sun heats up water in oceans, lakes, and rivers. The water turns into an invisible gas called water vapor and floats up into the sky!

🌞 You've Seen This!

Example 1: When you take a bath and the mirror gets foggy—that's water vapor!

Example 2: When a puddle dries up on a sunny day—the water evaporated into the air!

Example 3: When wet clothes on the clothesline dry—the water evaporated!

☁️💨

Step 2: Condensation

Say it: con-den-SAY-shun

What happens: When water vapor goes high up in the sky, it gets cold! The cold makes the water vapor turn back into tiny water droplets. These droplets stick together and make clouds!

☁️ You've Seen This!

Example 1: When you see your breath on a cold day—that's condensation!

Example 2: When a cold glass of lemonade has water drops on the outside—that's condensation!

Example 3: Clouds in the sky are made of condensation!

🌧️❄️

Step 3: Precipitation

Say it: prih-sip-ih-TAY-shun

What happens: When clouds get too full of water droplets, the water falls back down! This can be rain, snow, sleet, or hail!

🌧️ You've Seen This!

Example 1: Rain falling from the clouds!

Example 2: Snow in winter!

Example 3: Hail (little ice balls) during a storm!

All of these are precipitation—just different forms of water falling from the sky!

🌊🏞️

Step 4: Collection

Say it: kuh-LEK-shun

What happens: Water that falls as rain or snow collects in oceans, lakes, rivers, and even underground! Then the sun heats it up again, and the cycle starts over!

🌊 You've Seen This!

Example 1: Rain collecting in puddles!

Example 2: Rivers flowing to the ocean!

Example 3: Water in lakes and ponds!

💙 You're doing GREAT! Those are big science words, but you just learned them! Evaporation, Condensation, Precipitation, Collection. Say them out loud—you sound like a real scientist! 🥼🔬

🎮 Let's Practice!

Which step of the water cycle is happening? Think about your answer!

Situation 1:

🌞 It's a sunny day. A puddle from yesterday's rain is getting smaller and smaller until it's gone!

What's happening? (Think before you look!)

Answer: Evaporation! The sun is heating the water and turning it into water vapor that goes up into the air!

Situation 2:

☁️ You look up at the sky and see fluffy white clouds!

What's happening?

Answer: Condensation! Water vapor cooled down high in the sky and turned into tiny droplets that made clouds!

Situation 3:

🌧️ It's raining! Water is falling from the clouds onto the ground!

What's happening?

Answer: Precipitation! The clouds got too heavy with water, so the water is falling down as rain!

Situation 4:

🏞️ The rain from the storm is filling up the lake near your house!

What's happening?

Answer: Collection! The water is gathering together in the lake, where it will wait for the sun to heat it up and start the cycle again!

💙 How did you do? If you got them all right—awesome! If you missed some—that's totally fine! Learning means practicing, and you're practicing right now! Try reading through the steps again if you need to. You've got this! 💪

🎉 Amazing Water Facts!

🦕 Fact 1: Dinosaur Water!

The water on Earth today is the SAME water that was here when dinosaurs lived! The water cycle has been going round and round for billions of years! The water you drank today might have been in a T-Rex's footprint once! 🦖💧

🌍 Fact 2: Never-Ending Supply!

We will NEVER run out of water because of the water cycle! The same water keeps going round and round. But we DO need to keep water clean so it's safe to drink! 💙

☁️ Fact 3: Clouds are Heavy!

A cloud that looks fluffy and light can actually weigh as much as 100 elephants! 🐘🐘🐘 That's a LOT of water droplets!

💧 Fact 4: Water is Everywhere!

Most of Earth (about 70%) is covered in water! But most of that is salty ocean water. Only a tiny bit (about 3%) is fresh water we can drink! That's why we need to save water and not waste it! 🌊

🧪 BONUS: Try This Experiment at Home!

Make Your Own Mini Water Cycle!

You'll need:

  • A clear plastic cup or bowl
  • Some water
  • A sunny window
  • A grown-up to help (optional)
  1. Step 1: Put some water in the cup (about halfway full)
  2. Step 2: Put the cup in a sunny window
  3. Step 3: Wait a few hours or a day
  4. Step 4: Look at the cup! Is there less water? Where did it go?
  5. Step 5: Can you see tiny water drops on the sides of the cup? That's condensation!

🌟 You just made a mini water cycle! The water evaporated, turned into water vapor, then condensed on the sides! If you left it long enough, drops might even fall back down (precipitation)!
